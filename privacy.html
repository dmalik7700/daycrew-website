Privacy Notice

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who we are

DayCrew Services is a business partnership of Muhammad Daud Malik and Mahmoud Sabek. DayCrew Services is responsible for the personal information described in this notice.

Privacy enquiries: admin@daycrew.co.uk

What information we collect

When you contact DayCrew, request a quote or book a service, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, job address or postcode, photographs, preferred dates, access and parking information, details about the work you want us to carry out, communications with us, and quote, booking, invoice or payment information.

Please avoid sending personal information that is not relevant to the job.

Why we use it

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, arrange and deliver services, communicate with you about a job, issue invoices and record payments, maintain business and tax records, deal with questions, complaints or safety issues, protect the business against misuse or fraud, and meet legal obligations.

Our lawful bases

Depending on the circumstances, we process personal information because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract or to perform our contract with you; because we have a legal obligation to keep or use certain information; or because we have a legitimate interest in operating and protecting DayCrew Services, maintaining appropriate records, managing safety issues and establishing or defending legal claims, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.

Website quote form

Our website quote form uses Basin to receive and process submissions for DayCrew. Information entered into the form, including any uploaded photographs, is therefore processed through Basin for the purpose of delivering and managing your enquiry.

Who we share information with

We do not sell customer information. Where reasonably necessary, information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the business, such as our website hosting provider, online form provider, email provider, banking or payment provider, bookkeeping or accounting provider, and professional advisers. We may also disclose information where required by law.

How long we keep information

We do not keep personal information indefinitely. Quote enquiries that do not become bookings are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the enquiry and any related follow-up. Booking, invoice, payment and other business records may be kept for longer where necessary for tax, accounting and legal purposes.

Information may also be retained for longer where reasonably necessary in connection with an unresolved complaint, dispute, legal claim or legal requirement.

Photographs

Photographs submitted for quotes should relate only to the work you are asking DayCrew to carry out. Where possible, please avoid including people, personal documents or unrelated private information in photographs.

Photographs will only be used for purposes connected with the enquiry, booking, safety assessment or a related dispute unless another use has separately been agreed.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure and limit access to people and service providers who reasonably need it for the operation of DayCrew.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, data-protection law may give you rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, request deletion or restriction in certain circumstances, object to certain uses, and receive certain information in a portable format where applicable.

To make a privacy request, contact admin@daycrew.co.uk.

Complaints

If you are concerned about how your personal information has been handled, please contact us first so we can try to resolve the issue. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes to this notice

We may update this privacy notice where our services, systems or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this website.
