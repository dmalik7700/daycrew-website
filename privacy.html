Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 2026

What information we collect

When you contact DayCrew or request a quote, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, job address or postcode, photographs, access information and details about the work you want us to carry out.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, arrange and deliver services, maintain business records, deal with questions or complaints, and meet legal or tax obligations.

How this website form works

The quote form on this website prepares an email in your own email application. The form itself does not store your submission on the website. Your information is sent only when you choose to send the resulting email.

Who we share information with

We do not sell customer information. We may share information where reasonably necessary with service providers we use to operate the business or where required by law.

How long we keep information

We keep business and customer records only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above and to meet applicable legal, tax or record-keeping obligations.

Your rights

You may contact us to ask about the personal information we hold about you and to exercise any data-protection rights that apply to you.
