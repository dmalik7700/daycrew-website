Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 2026

What information we collect

When you contact DayCrew or request a quote, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, job address or postcode, photographs, access information and details about the work you want us to carry out.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, arrange and deliver services, maintain business records, deal with questions or complaints, and meet legal or tax obligations.

Website quote form

Our website quote form uses FormSubmit to transmit submissions to DayCrew by email. Information entered into the form, including any uploaded photographs, is therefore processed through that service for the purpose of delivering your enquiry to us.

Who we share information with

We do not sell customer information. We may use service providers that help us operate the website, email and business administration, and we may disclose information where required by law.

How long we keep information

We keep customer and business records only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant enquiry, service, dispute, accounting or legal requirement.

Your rights

You may contact us to ask about the personal information we hold about you and to exercise any data-protection rights that apply to you.
