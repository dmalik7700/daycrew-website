HOW THE QUOTE FORM WORKS
------------------------
This site is completely static and needs no paid website backend.

When a customer completes the quote form and presses "Prepare my quote request",
their email app opens a message addressed to info@daycrew.co.uk.

The form details are automatically inserted into the email. The customer can
then attach photos or video before sending.

IMPORTANT BEFORE GOING PUBLIC
-----------------------------
1. Check the pricing and wording one final time.
2. Add your chosen public/service address wherever legally appropriate.
3. Review the customer terms and privacy notice against your final setup.
4. If you later add analytics, pixels, embedded maps, chat widgets or other
   tracking tools, update the privacy/cookie setup.
5. Test the site on phone and desktop.
6. Send a test quote enquiry before launch.

FREE HOSTING
------------
This site can be hosted free on GitHub Pages or another static-site host.
The next step is to upload these files to the free host and point daycrew.co.uk
to it through the IONOS DNS settings.

You do NOT need to buy an IONOS website-builder subscription for these files.
