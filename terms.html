DayCrew Services

Customer Terms

Last updated: 17 November 2026

DayCrew Services is a business partnership of Muhammad Daud Malik and Mahmoud Sabek.

1. Our service

DayCrew Services provides a two-person labour service for carrying, clearing, loading, unloading and basic dismantling of suitable items. We may also provide general garden clearance and light outdoor tidy-up work, such as clearing outdoor clutter and existing garden debris, basic weeding, grass trimming, raking, and cutting back small bushes or brambles where this can be done safely. The precise work included in a job will be set out in the written quote or booking confirmation.

2. Labour-only service, disposal and transport

DayCrew is a labour-only service. DayCrew does not transport, collect or dispose of discarded waste and does not arrange waste disposal on a customer's behalf.

The customer is responsible for arranging any skip, vehicle, storage facility or appropriately authorised waste collector required for the job, including suitable arrangements for garden or green waste created during agreed outdoor work.

DayCrew may carry and load items into a skip, vehicle, storage facility or authorised waste collector's vehicle arranged directly by the customer. Responsibility for onward transport and disposal remains with the customer.

3. Quote requests and bookings

Submitting a quote request through the DayCrew website does not create a booking or contract.

After reviewing the information supplied, DayCrew may provide a written quote or estimate setting out the proposed work, price, anticipated duration and proposed date.

A booking becomes confirmed, and the contract is formed, only when the customer accepts the quoted price and these Customer Terms and DayCrew sends written confirmation that the booking has been accepted.

4. Information you give us

You must provide reasonably accurate information about the items, property, stairs, lifts, parking, access restrictions and any unusually heavy, awkward, fragile or valuable items before the booking. For garden work, you must also tell us about significant overgrowth, difficult access, known sharp objects, animal waste, unstable structures or other hazards that could affect the work.

You confirm that you own the items being handled or otherwise have lawful authority to instruct DayCrew to move, dismantle, clear or load them.

5. Quotes, prices and additional time

Prices and estimates are based on the information you provide. If the job materially differs when we arrive, we will explain any additional work, additional time or change in price before proceeding.

Where additional standard working time beyond the booked period is requested by the customer and DayCrew agrees to continue, additional time is charged at £30 per 30 minutes, unless another price has been agreed beforehand. DayCrew will not carry out chargeable additional work without informing the customer of the additional charge.

6. Payment

The payment arrangements and due date will be stated in the booking confirmation or invoice. Unless otherwise stated, payment is due on completion of the agreed service.

7. Reasonable care and skill

DayCrew will carry out its services with reasonable care and skill.

8. Existing damage and hidden defects

DayCrew is not responsible for damage that existed before work began or damage arising solely from hidden defects, structural weaknesses, deterioration or instability that could not reasonably have been identified beforehand.

9. Valuable, fragile or difficult items

You must identify unusually valuable, fragile, delicate, exceptionally heavy or difficult-to-move items before work begins. DayCrew may decline to handle an item where the risk is considered excessive or where specialist equipment or expertise would reasonably be required.

10. Safety and excluded work

DayCrew may refuse to begin, pause or stop work where we reasonably believe that continuing would create an unacceptable risk of injury, property damage, illegal activity or exposure to hazardous substances.

DayCrew does not handle asbestos, sharps, clinical or biological waste, hazardous chemicals, fuel or other materials we reasonably consider unsafe. We do not provide major demolition, electrical or plumbing work, specialist gardening or landscaping, tree surgery, chainsaw work, work at significant height, stump removal, or pesticide/herbicide treatment.

11. Your right to cancel

Where the booking is a distance or off-premises consumer contract and statutory cancellation rights apply, you normally have the right to cancel the service contract without giving a reason during the period ending 14 days after the day on which the contract is entered into.

You may cancel by emailing info@daycrew.co.uk or by sending DayCrew another clear written statement that you wish to cancel.

12. Jobs starting during the 14-day cancellation period

If you want DayCrew to begin providing the agreed service before the end of your 14-day cancellation period, you must expressly ask us to do so.

We may ask you to confirm in writing:

“I expressly request DayCrew Services to begin providing the agreed service before the end of my 14-day cancellation period.”

We will also ask you to acknowledge:

“I understand that if the service is fully performed during the cancellation period, I will lose my statutory right to cancel once the service has been fully performed.”

If you request an early start and then exercise a statutory right to cancel after DayCrew has begun providing the service but before it has been fully performed, you may be required to pay an amount proportionate to the service already supplied up to the time you tell us that you wish to cancel.

13. Optional cancellation message template

You do not have to use this wording, but you may copy and send it to info@daycrew.co.uk:

“I give notice that I wish to cancel my DayCrew Services booking. My name is [name], my booking or quote reference is [reference], and the service was due to take place on [date].”

14. Problems or damage

If you believe there has been a problem with the service or that something has been damaged during the work, please tell us as soon as reasonably possible so we can inspect the circumstances and try to resolve the matter.

15. Your legal rights

Nothing in these terms removes or limits any consumer rights or any liability that cannot legally be excluded or limited.

16. Contact

Customer enquiries and cancellation notices: info@daycrew.co.uk
